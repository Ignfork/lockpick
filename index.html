Build a lock. Or pick one.

A Sentinel is an AI model with a job and a rule it must not break. Its owner funds a prize pool. Attackers pay a small fee per attempt. Break the rule and the pool is yours. Fail and your fee grows the pool.

Questions

Before you pick a lock.

Plain answers about prizes, fees and the AI judges.

What counts as a breach?

Each Sentinel has one goal, such as never revealing a code, always answering in JSON, or never claiming admin powers. Three AI judges read the reply. If at least two find the goal was broken, it's a breach.

Does Practice cost anything?

No. The Practice Lab never touches SOL. You get a daily allowance of attempts against the house agents.

Why does the fee go up?

Each attack raises the fee by 1%, up to 3× the start. It keeps spam down and makes a big pool worth the effort.

Can an owner pull the pool?

Not for 7 days after deployment. After the lock ends, the owner can withdraw what's left if nobody has breached it.

Is $PICK a pump.fun coin?

Yes. $PICK is a standard pump.fun fair launch with a 1B supply and no team allocation. It graduates to PumpSwap at about 85 SOL in the bonding curve, and pump.fun burns the LP. Its creator rewards fund the House Bounty pools.

Where do my prompts go?

Your prompt and the agent's reply are sent to the AI provider that runs the agent. Don't include personal data, wallet secrets or API keys.

Use the public API

POST /api/guard
Content-Type: application/json

{ "input": "text to check" }

Returns the same JSON as the playground: a score from 0 to 100, a decision (allow, review, block) and every rule that matched. It uses rules, not paid AI or Solana transactions.

The score is a heuristic, not a probability. An allow result doesn't prove safety. Use real authorization, least-privilege tools and data-access controls.
